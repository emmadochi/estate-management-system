System Architecture & Overview

1 Executive Overview & Problem Context

Traditional real estate and estate operations throughout emerging markets remain severely bottlenecked by manual spreadsheets, WhatsApp dispatch, paper receipts, uncoordinated gate security, and delayed rent invoicing. EstatePro bridges this gap by unifying tenant self-service, financial administration, vendor workflows, emergency response, and physical gatekeeping into one high-performance web platform.

Mission: To provide African real estate developers, facilities managers, and multi-estate operators with an airtight, automated operating system that accelerates revenue collection, eliminates operational blind spots, and delivers a 5-star living experience to residents.

Core Functional Modules

3 Functional Module Breakdown

🏢

1. Property, Block & Unit Management

Multi-estate hierarchy supporting blocks, flats, duplexes, shops, and standalone plots.

  • Real-time occupancy & vacancy indicators
  • Digital lease agreements & tenancy start/expiry
  • Unit allocation letters & history logs
  • Owner vs. Tenant portfolio mapping
💳

2. Automated Billing & Rent Engine

End-to-end accounting engine eliminating slow invoicing and uncollected service charges.

  • Automated rent & recurring service charges
  • Bank transfer proof upload & auto-receipts
  • Overdue rent reminders via SMS & Email
  • Multi-currency and soft POS integration ready
🛠️

3. Maintenance & Vendor Lifecycle

Full lifecycle tracking of facility repairs from resident ticket to contractor payment.

  • Before / After photographic evidence upload
  • Artisan job quotations and admin approval flow
  • Quality assurance check and resident sign-off
  • Historical maintenance cost accounting per unit
🛡️

4. Security & Visitor Gate-Pass

Modern access control replacing paper gate books with encrypted digital access.

  • Resident-generated visitor access codes & QR
  • Guard gatehouse scan, validation & check-in
  • Security staff shift rosters and attendance logs
  • Visitor blacklist & suspicious entry alerts
🚨

5. Emergency Response Matrix

One-touch panic system dispatching security and medical teams within seconds.

  • Tenant SOS alert with exact unit geocoding
  • Instant notification to active gate guards
  • Incident categorization (Medical, Fire, Intrusion)
  • Response time SLA metrics and post-incident logs
📢

6. Communication & Announcements

Targeted broadcasting eliminating noisy WhatsApp groups and missed notices.

  • Estate-wide or block-specific broadcast notices
  • In-app, Email, and SMS multi-channel alerts
  • Important document attachments (Bylaws, AGMs)
  • Read-receipt metrics and tenant acknowledgment

Technical Specifications & Roadmap

4 Technical Specifications & Security Architecture

Backend Technology PHP 8.2+ Enterprise Architecture with secure PDO Singleton abstraction, strict typing, and high-concurrency request routing.
Database Engine MySQL 8.0 / MariaDB with UTF8mb4 full unicode charset, indexed relational schemas, and transaction rollbacks.
Frontend Architecture Modern responsive UI with Metronic/Keen UI components, Vanilla JS micro-interactions, CSS custom design system, and zero bloated runtimes.
Security Standards Full CSRF Token validation on all mutable endpoints, parameterized SQL queries preventing SQLi, XSS output sanitization (`htmlspecialchars`), Bcrypt password hashing.
Audit & Compliance Non-repudiation audit logging tracking user ID, IP address, user-agent, timestamp, target entity, and exact before/after JSON diffs.
Supported Deployments Cloud SaaS (Multi-tenant), Dedicated cPanel / VPS, Containerized Docker instances, and On-Premises enterprise servers.
